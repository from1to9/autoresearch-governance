# Recovery prompt

```text
Recover <task-record> within current human instructions and the active lease.
Read own binding/contacts, compact checkpoint, relevant new events and artifacts
needed for the next action. Resolve a real conflict at its authoritative source;
do not infer the goal from the last fragment or replay full history.

Confirm scope, owner, pending decision and next action. Preserve valid evidence,
completed stages and compatible partial work. Repair at the failed boundary and
resume through the native contract; verify consumption and new progress when due.
Do not invalidate work for a new SHA or missing auxiliary metadata alone.

Update existing state with material findings and continue authorized work.
No new task, resource, message or runtime authority follows from recovery itself.
```
